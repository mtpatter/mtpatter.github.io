The Children's Museum of Indianapolis · June to September 2026

Lechleiter Indiana Visiting Artist Program - Joy of Carving Workshops

Visiting artist: Maria Patterson

Participants~285

children and families were introduced to the art of subtractive sculpture through soap carving activities and created works of art of their own.

18workshop sessions
9workshop days over four months
~200kids, about 70% of all participants
~85parents and other adults carving alongside them
Reach

About 16 people per session, June through September

Workshops ran as afternoon drop-in sessions for museum visitors, plus dedicated sessions for two of the museum's youth programs. June was the biggest month because it included three StarPoint Summer Camp groups.

Kids and teens Adults
Who took part

Museum visitors, summer campers and teen apprentices

Drop-in sessions ~200

Visitors across 14 public sessions, mostly families working side by side.

StarPoint Summer Camp ~60

Campers in three groups. StarPoint is the museum's low-cost summer camp for neighborhood kids going into grades 1–6.

Museum Apprentice Program 22

Teens in the museum's skill-building program for ages 13–18, in a dedicated September session.

Demand

People sought the workshop out

  • One August session filled entirely through pre-registration, so no one had to be recruited from the hallway.

  • One family told us they came to the museum that day specifically to make sure they could do the workshop.

  • A family traveling between Chicago and Cincinnati stopped at the museum, and the mom said the workshop was one of the best parts of their trip.

  • A visitor recognized the artist's carving work from Instagram and stopped to say they liked it.

In their words
